# Prodify

A productivity app for music producers: they time their sessions and watch their skills grow.

## Sessions

**Session**:
One timed stretch of music work. The producer starts it and usually stops it; Prodify stops it after eight hours if it is still running.
_Avoid_: Run, block, entry

**Session type**:
What kind of work a session is, e.g. Mixing or Learning. Every session has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Category, mode

**Production session**:
A session of the type Production, where the producer works across many areas on one track.
_Avoid_: Full session, mixed session

## Skills

**Area**:
A top-level field of music production, such as Beat Making, Mixing or Mastering. Each has its own level.
_Avoid_: Branch (in copy), category, discipline

**Focus**:
A specific skill inside one area, such as EQ inside Mixing.
_Avoid_: Sub-skill, subgroup, tag

**Intention**:
A focus chosen when a session starts; at most two per session.
_Avoid_: Goal, plan

**Reflection**:
The producer's account, after a session, of which areas and focuses it trained.
_Avoid_: Review, log

**Main focus**:
The one focus of a session with the most assigned time.
_Avoid_: Primary skill, star

**Assigned time**:
The minutes the producer puts on one focus after a session. The sum cannot exceed the session's duration.
_Avoid_: Area weight, share, percentage, a little / some / a lot

**Unassigned time**:
The part of the session's duration that was not put on any focus. It does not grow the tree.
_Avoid_: Remainder, leftover, slack

**Skill time**:
The part of a session's duration credited to an area or focus; it drives their levels. It is the assigned time, or an even split when nothing was assigned.
_Avoid_: XP, points

## Copy

**Studio voice**:
Producer-facing messages talk like studio talk: Session, Streak, Area, Focus, Skill time. English, short, factual.
_Avoid_: Branch, block, XP (except rank), emoji in system messages, UTC, coach slogans
